**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

**Reviewer roles:**

## Problem

Which problem requires a change? Name the requirement that is wrong, the
limit that has to move, the scope that has to shift, or the honest limit of
the product that has to be corrected.

## Requested Change

What is requested, in one sentence?

## Reason

Why now, and why this way? A change that is only convenient is not a reason.

## Impacted Requirement IDs

The identifiers of the requirements that change. A requirement that is
removed is named with its identifier, and the reason is written down.

## Impacted Architecture

The crates, the interfaces and the boundaries that change. A change of the
build contract is named explicitly.

## Impacted Platforms

The platforms that are affected, and the platforms that are not.

## Milestone Impact

The milestone and the gate that change. A change that moves a milestone says
where the work goes instead.

## Acceptance / Test Changes

The acceptance criteria that change, and the tests that are added, changed or
removed. A weakening of a criterion is named as such.

## Risks

What can go wrong, how likely it is, and what the mitigation is.

## Decision

The decision, the date, and the role that took it. A proposal is not a
decision: the decision is recorded here, after the review.
